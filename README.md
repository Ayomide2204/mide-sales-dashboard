This data analysis project aims to provide insights into the sales performance of an e-commerce company over the past year. By analyzing various aspects of the sales data, we seek to identify recommendations, and gain a deeper understanding of the company`s perfromance.

### Data Sources

sales data: The primary dataset used for this analysis is the "ecommerce_orders_dataset.xlsx" file, containing detailed information about each sale made by the company.

EDA involved exploring the sales data to answer key questions , such as;

### Results findings

The analysis results are summarized as follows
1. The company`s total sum of quantity has steadily been increasing over the past year.
2. Total count of customer id was identified.
3. The company`s total revenue improved by surpassing the target for the year.
4. The customer rating identifies that the average rating was above average and exceeds the expectation.

### Recommendations

Based on the analysis, we recommend the following actions;
- Expand on products varieties to boost revenue.
- Capitalize on marketing and promotions.
- Start a program that focuses on appreciating top customers every month which can help improve the amount of returning customers.
